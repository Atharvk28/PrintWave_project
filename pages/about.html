OUR STORY

More Than Just Printing.

PrintWave was created around a simple idea: clothing and everyday products should give people a way to express themselves.

Whether it's your favourite quote, a memorable photograph, your college team, a festival design, or your own artwork, we make it possible to turn that idea into a personalized product.

From printed and embroidered T-shirts to scarves and personalized mugs, PrintWave brings customization into one simple online experience.
